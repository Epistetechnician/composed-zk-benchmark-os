quarantine-resolution escalation-blocker review boundary`.

review metadata over one Phase 459 tiny-Z3 accepted-append decision

## Future Review Purpose

The future escalation-blocker review may record why one Phase 459 escalation
blocker is well formed, digest-bound, and still non-promotional. It may only
summarize escalation-blocker consistency, Phase 457 resolution-review
consistency, local Z3 replay binding, accepted-append blocker consistency,
nonclaim completeness, and promotion safety.

The future review is not an accepted append decision. It is not an accepted
Evidence Ledger mutation and does not create accepted formal evidence.
