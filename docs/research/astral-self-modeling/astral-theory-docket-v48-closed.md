## Common reopening contract

Every candidate must define, before data or effects are opened:

1. causal variables, assignment, timing, consistency, positivity, and
   no-interference assumptions;
2. a causal pathway and intervention operator that can be directly measured;
3. a genuinely new estimand rather than a relabeling of V46 or V48;
4. an exact effect threshold, uncertainty rule, missingness rule, and
   multiplicity correction;
5. a power and reliability analysis tied to the independent unit;
6. falsifiers and terminal stop rules without adaptive model, layer, wrapper,
   position, threshold, corpus, feature, or control selection;
7. fresh data identity, document/concept-disjoint splits, unchanged rival
   controls, prediction locking before assessment effects, aggregate-only
   retention, and independent validation; and
8. if multiple models are claimed, a frozen model panel and model-specific
   operators chosen before results, with model interaction analyzed rather than
   searched for a positive.

Failure to satisfy any item rejects the proposal and leaves Astral stopped.

## Rejection rules

Reject a proposal if it:

- chooses a model, layer, wrapper, position, alpha, threshold, corpus, or
  feature after observing results;
- treats V18, V25, V27, V30-V38, or V48 instrument/governance results as
  evidence of introspection or causal self-modeling;
- reuses V25, V28-V29, V39-V48 scientific artifacts as future inputs rather
  than rationale-only historical context;
- substitutes decodability, correlation, graph plausibility, language-model
  improvement, or monitor behavior for a directly measured causal effect;
- pools dependent model or family observations without an explicit uncertainty
  and multiplicity model;
- opens assessment before prediction locking and independent review; or
- raises the claim ceiling above the named bounded target without a new gate.
