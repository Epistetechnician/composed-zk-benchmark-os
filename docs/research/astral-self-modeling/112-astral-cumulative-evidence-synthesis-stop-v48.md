## Evidence boundary

The synthesis distinguishes four kinds of repository state:

1. **Instrument feasibility.** Several controlled seams became usable and
   reproducible. V27 established final-embedding intervention feasibility;
   V38 established the Qwen3.6 layer-capture/replacement feasibility boundary;
   V39-V48 repeatedly passed the relevant custody, parity, shape, repeat, or
   replacement prerequisites when those prerequisites were in scope.
2. **Governance and measurement controls.** V30-V37 validated planted-circuit,
   scoring, custody, replay, and execution-eligibility controls. These are
   safeguards and validation machinery, not scientific evidence for Astral.
3. **Bounded information or development results.** V25 is an information-
   presence/report-gap result only. V18 is a bounded trained input-ablation
   development candidate with explicit task-visibility and cross-model
   limitations. Neither result establishes activation access, introspection, or
   causal self-modeling.
4. **Scientific target tests.** The directly measured target-validity lanes
   repeatedly failed their preregistered gates. These failures are informative
   about the tested operationalizations, but they do not prove that no internal
   state exists in any model or architecture.

## Theory audit

Any theory capable of reopening Astral must define, before data or effects are
opened:

- the causal variables, assignment mechanism, timing, consistency,
  positivity, and no-interference assumptions;
- the intervention and the pathway by which the proposed state can affect the
  measured outcome;
- a genuinely new estimand, not a relabeling of V46 or V48;
- the exact effect threshold, uncertainty interval, missingness rule, and
  multiplicity correction;
- a power and target-reliability analysis tied to the independent unit;
- falsifiers that stop the study without adaptive layer, wrapper, position,
  threshold, corpus, model, or control selection; and
- the model role. If architecture generalization is claimed, the model panel
  and all model-specific operators must be frozen before results and analyzed
  as a predeclared factor rather than searched for a positive result.

The currently discussed ideas do not yet meet that bar:

- **Recirculation** is a candidate state-persistence mechanism. It would need
  a fixed recurrence intervention and a causal mediation or accessibility
  estimand showing that the recirculated state changes a held-out outcome beyond
  text/input and matched controls. Language-model improvement alone is not
  evidence of self-modeling.
- **Circuit tracing** is a hypothesis-generation and localization scaffold.
  Attribution or replacement graphs would still require direct intervention in
  the original model and held-out effect prediction; a readable graph is not
  causal validation.
- **J-space or workspace-style representations** are representational
  hypotheses. Decodability or broadcast-like geometry is insufficient without
  intervention sensitivity, causal localization, and rival-control separation.
- **Neural Chameleons** are a separate monitor-evasion branch. The V82 missing
  artifact bundle cannot be substituted with Qwen3.6 or Astral artifacts and
  cannot be used to reopen Astral.
- **A multi-model panel** is valuable only when the theory predicts
  cross-architecture transport. It must be an independently frozen replication
  design, not model shopping after the Qwen3.6 null.

No candidate theory currently supplies all required operational details. The
theory audit therefore does not authorize a model panel, new corpus, or
measurement run.

## Binding stop rules

The following are closed under this synthesis:

- no V48 retuning or reopening;
- no new model, layer, wrapper, alpha, position, threshold, or corpus variation
  as a response to V48;
- no assessment effects or new scientific execution;
- no reuse of V25, V28-V29, V39-V48 scientific artifacts as inputs to a future
  study;
- no treatment of V30-V37 as scientific evidence;
- no V61 or V82 evidence sharing;
- no Stage 0C or Stage 1 promotion; and
- no introspection, causal self-modeling, benchmark, consciousness, or
  production-readiness claim.

The corrected V48 aggregate roots remain authoritative. The pre-correction
measurement pair is superseded and excluded from evidence. Raw prompts,
tokens, activations, logits, generated text, transcripts, credentials, and
per-family arrays remain outside the retained V48 aggregate result.

## Reopening gate

Astral may reopen only through a separately authorized state slice whose design
memo is independently reviewed and accepted before implementation or effects.
The memo must contain the complete theory-audit fields above, a fresh data
identity, a sealed implementation contract naming the actor, runtime, operator,
runner, validator, custody root, and claim ceiling, unchanged rival controls,
prediction locking before assessment effects, aggregate-only retention, and an
independent validation plan.

If no materially stronger theory meets that review, Astral experimentation is
terminated rather than extended through another parameter variation.

The maintained unexecuted hypothesis register is the [Astral theory
docket](astral-theory-docket-v48-closed.md). It is a review queue only and does
not authorize any docket entry.
