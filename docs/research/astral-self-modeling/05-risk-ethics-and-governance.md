# Risk, Ethics, and Governance

## Research Risks

| Risk | Control | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| Anthropomorphic overclaiming | Use operational definitions; prohibit consciousness and subjective-experience inference | Reporting equates a metric with sentience or complete self-knowledge |
| Confident mechanistic storytelling | Calibration loss, abstention, held-out interventions, and error examples | Verbal quality rises while causal fidelity falls |
| Tracer Goodharting | Frozen actor first, independent auditor, alternate tracer, telemetry bottleneck | Predictability rises without audited causal accuracy |
| Dual use for concealment or control | Do not optimize against monitor evasion; restrict sensitive telemetry and adversarial findings | Work materially enables evasion without a reviewed mitigation purpose |
| Privacy leakage from activations or prompts | Data minimization, access control, retention limits, redaction review | Secrets, personal data, or prohibited training data enter artifacts |
| Safety regression from self-correction | Independent safety suite and domain-specific reports | Material safety degradation at any claimed performance gain |
| Misleading novelty claims | Systematic literature matrix and qualified language | Equivalent prior work is omitted or candidate novelty is stated as fact |
| Irreproducible model behavior | Pin checkpoints, seeds, code, data, tracer, and hardware | Required provenance or licenses are unavailable |

## Human Review Gates

Human review is required before:

1. collecting or retaining raw activations from sensitive inputs;
2. moving beyond public or approved datasets;
3. training the actor rather than only the observer;
4. publishing dual-use intervention or monitor-evasion findings;
5. changing a ledger claim to `Not refuted`;
6. describing any result as novel, faithful, causal, or safety-relevant.

## Reporting Rules

- Publish nulls, negative results, failed replications, and excluded cases.
- Distinguish tracer agreement from ground-truth causal accuracy.
- Report subgroup and task-family failures rather than only averages.
- Keep capability gain separate from safety and calibration.
- Attribute every external claim to a source with a stated limit.
- Treat the shared conversation as provenance for ideas, not evidence.

## Data Governance

Before data collection, record data owner, license, intended use, retention
period, access group, permitted outputs, deletion procedure, and whether
activations could reproduce sensitive input information. Default to the minimum
telemetry needed for the registered hypothesis.
