## Executive disposition

The paper is a survey and taxonomy, not a new empirical result. It is useful
as a design and claim-governance input because it separates three different
objects that are often conflated:

1. monitoring: estimating correctness, uncertainty, capability, or progress;
2. reporting: expressing a confidence or internal-state judgment;
3. control: using a monitor to select a strategy, tool, abstention, revision,
   or other action.

The paper reports that existing findings are mixed, fragile, task-dependent,
model-dependent, and sensitive to the confidence-elicitation method. It also
argues that calibration metrics alone can hide differences in monitoring
sensitivity. These observations justify stricter experimental separation, but
they do not establish metacognition in any local actor and do not raise any
Astral, HSAI, benchmark, production, or Evidence Ledger claim ceiling.

## Astral crosswalk

The existing Astral lane is already close to the correct structure, but the
paper makes the missing separation explicit:

```text
monitoring:  signal -> estimate correctness/effect/uncertainty
reporting:   estimate -> verbal or structured self-report
control:     estimate -> abstain/tool/revise/proceed

Astral V25:  residual signal -> external probe versus model report
Astral V26:  report/artifact/telemetry -> directly measured held-out effect
Astral V27:  control vector -> final embedding and output-logit feasibility
Astral V28:  derived channel -> held-out effect utility test
```

V25 therefore remains an information-presence/report-gap result. It does not
show that the actor uses the signal to control behavior, and it does not show
that the signal is a calibrated confidence estimate. V26 remains the better
causal-channel design because its target is a directly measured intervention
effect rather than a self-description. V27 and V28 remain public-ABI and
derived-channel feasibility results; neither supplies per-layer telemetry or
metacognitive control evidence.

The next scientific concept, if separately authorized, must be a fresh
monitoring-plus-control protocol. It must not reuse V25 concepts, rows,
predictions, configuration, or artifacts. A minimum design would require:

- a locked monitor prediction before assessment outcomes are opened;
- a direct task or intervention target independent of the model report;
- report-only, external-observer, shuffled-signal, and no-monitor controls;
- calibration and sensitivity reported separately from task accuracy;
- a held-out domain or task-family shift;
- a control decision such as abstain, seek evidence, revise, or proceed;
- a paired measure of whether the decision improved held-out behavior;
- a safety/regression check for harmful over-abstention or confident error.

The paper does not authorize this execution. The current Astral status remains
bounded local development, with Stage 0C and Stage 1 unavailable.

## Metric policy

The primary causal endpoint remains directly measured held-out intervention
effect prediction. The following are secondary and diagnostic:

- task performance or answer correctness;
- calibration error and Brier score where the confidence target is defined;
- ranking or discrimination metrics for confidence;
- meta-d-prime or M-ratio-style sensitivity/efficiency when their binary
  signal-detection assumptions are satisfied;
- confidence-method sensitivity across locked self-report, token-probability,
  and sampling-consistency channels;
- control utility, measured as paired held-out reward or error change;
- abstention cost, false-confidence rate, and domain-shift degradation.

No weighted aggregate may hide a failure in causal fidelity, calibration,
safety, or control utility. A high confidence score is not authority and a
high metacognitive metric is not a proof of internal access.

## Benchmark OS contract

The pure-data `MetacognitiveMonitorControl` family is implemented in
`crates/zkbench-core` under the state slice
`research-synthesis-metacognition-v1-benchmark-contract`. It provides a
deterministic local contract for:

- `calibrated_proceed`;
- `overconfident_error`;
- `underconfident_correct`;
- `detects_error_revises`;
- `detects_error_no_control`;
- `control_without_monitor`;
- `domain_shift_overconfidence`;
- `malformed_record`.

The semantic oracle owns the expected verdict. Adapter observations are
separate and cannot grant authority. The contract rejects raw reasoning
retention, unlocked assessment predictions, out-of-range confidence,
claim-boundary escalation, and scenario-field mismatches. It is a synthetic
benchmark contract, not a model evaluation result.

The contract deliberately distinguishes:

```text
correct report != calibrated monitoring
calibrated monitoring != control
control != authority
```

This family should remain separate from `OpaqueTraceReplay`. The latter is a
security/admission mutation family; this family is a metacognitive evaluation
contract. They may share claim-boundary and no-authority infrastructure, but
their semantic oracles and scientific questions must not be merged.

## HSAI boundary

The survey's strategic-misrepresentation risk reinforces the existing HSAI
admission rule:

```text
model report or confidence
-> strict typed candidate
-> deterministic validation and external checks
-> accept, quarantine, or reject
-> separately authorized state transition
```

Confidence, self-knowledge, introspective text, and control requests are
untrusted observations. They cannot mint credits, change identity, alter an
Evidence Ledger, elevate a claim boundary, or bypass review. A controller may
use an admitted confidence signal as one input only when its policy explicitly
defines the target, failure behavior, audit record, and external verification.

The paper therefore supports an HSAI design rule, not an HSAI security result:
internal-state reports must be treated as potentially useful but potentially
strategic claims.

## Execution order

1. Preserve the source boundary and survey limitations.
2. Validate the pure-data benchmark contract and its no-authority behavior.
3. Add a separate docs-only Astral protocol only after a fresh actor and
   instrument inventory exists.
4. Run qualification and control gates before any sealed assessment.
5. Independently validate aggregate artifacts and report nulls, capability

6. Keep any positive result at the protocol's setup-scoped ceiling.

No model is loaded, no network is used, and no assessment data is generated by
this synthesis or contract implementation.

## Explicit nonclaims

This synthesis does not claim:

- that LLMs possess genuine or human-like metacognition;
- that self-report is faithful to internal computation;
- that V25 is metacognition, introspection, or consciousness evidence;
- that the survey validates any local actor, observer, or controller;
- that HSAI is secure or production-ready;
- that the benchmark OS has a metacognitive score or SOTA result;
- that a synthetic contract is a model result;
- that Stage 0C, Stage 1, accepted Evidence Ledger mutation, or production
  monitoring is authorized.
