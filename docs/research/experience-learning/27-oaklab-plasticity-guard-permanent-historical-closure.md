# Oak Lab plasticity-guard permanent historical closure

State slice: `oaklab-experience-learning-plasticity-guard-terminal-closure-v1`

Decision: permanently closed as a trainable or tunable mechanism in the Oak
Lab experience-learning lane.

The powered real-panel guard receipts remain valid negative historical
evidence. They may be cited as a fixed historical comparator, but they may not
be rerun, retuned, widened, narrowed, or used to select a successor mechanism.
Fixed SGD batch one is the active reference for all future scientific gates.

This closure prohibits:

- changing the guard floor, recovery, surprise threshold, optimizer, seeds,
  split boundaries, endpoints, or power assumptions;
- creating a new guard alias with equivalent behavior;
- treating rollback changes as a quality or adaptation improvement;
- running the guard on a fresh synthetic or real panel;
- mixing guard receipts with a later campaign manifest or energy trace;
- using any guard result as Astral evidence.

The implementation and existing receipts are preserved byte-for-byte as
historical context. A successor must use a materially different estimand and
state slice. This closure is not reversible by a positive result from another
mechanism.
