## Scientific object

The treatment is the complete lagged selective-credit trajectory, including
controller, model, pending, carryover, and resource state. It is compared with
fixed batch-one SGD on identical ordered streams and identical initial
checkpoints. The primary endpoint is paired post-washout loss difference. The
controller may read only the previous block; current-block outcomes cannot
affect action assignment.

Qualification streams are predictable signal plus noise, feature-relevance
drift, delayed reward, sparse events, long horizon, and pure noise. Real
execution, if separately authorized, requires fresh disjoint real stream
families and fit/tune/assessment separation.
