Competitive Moat

A risk score tells you the price of trusting an agent. An audit tells you where the code was weak. Neither tells you what the agent actually did, within what boundary, with evidence you can re-run. That is what this stack provides.

Competitors: Auditware (static analysis + audit tooling) · t54-labs (underwriting / risk scoring). This repo sells bounded, verifiable statements — claim packets with reproduction checkers.

Attack surface (honest)

  • Discipline is harder to demo than a single score → the forge makes it demoable.
  • Depth without surface: few packets → stamp M1 & M3 first.
  • Category may be won on speed by heuristics → track C5, stage differentiation packets.
  • Not a moat until exercised → the matrix + forge + ledger make it kinetic.
Nonclaims: this surface does not claim any catalyst will fire, that any module is production ready, that packaging creates or raises evidence, that competitors are inferior, or that any action grants authority or executes a backend. Level 0 design note only. Docs: docs/research/catalyst-strategy/
