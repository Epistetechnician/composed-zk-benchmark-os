00 / THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

Evidence discipline is the operating system

Everything below — every crate, every phase, every campaign — is an expression of one meta-rule. This is not a style choice; it is the thing being built. The repo treats an autonomous system's tendency to overclaim as the core failure mode, and engineers against it at every layer.

◈Named state-slice

Every mutation is a declared, scoped slice of state — never inferred from imports or convenience. What a phase may and may not touch is explicit and auditable.

⌘Preregistration

Experiments are specified — instruments, seeds, gates, stop-rules, interpretation ladder — before execution. Negative results are retained as first-class evidence.

▲Claim ceiling

Every artifact carries the strongest claim it may support. A benchmark pass is not a proof; a local replay is not official evidence; a recursion proof is not semantic proof.

⛓Append-only ledgers

Evidence, admission journals, and campaign state live in digest-chained, log-structured ledgers — durable, tamper-evident, never silently rewritten.

⊘Fail-closed gates

Validators reject by default: stale digests, unknown fields, promotion text, claim-boundary escalation. Absence of evidence is a hard stop, not a warning.

◉Sole mutation authority

In governed loops the mechanism (not the model, not the operator) is the only writer of state. Providers propose; the journal decides; humans audit.

Why this matters: the end-goal is autonomous systems that improve themselves. Unbounded, that is the alignment failure. Bounded by preregistration, evidence chains, and claim ceilings, it becomes a defensible engineering property. The whole repo is a proof that the bounded version is buildable.
01 / THE CLAIM LADDER

Seven rungs of evidence

Claims escalate only with artifacts. You cannot assert a higher rung without the evidence class beneath it. This ladder is enforced by validators across zkbench-core and hsai-claim-envelope, and it is the spine the rest of the repo hangs on.

L0
Design note
Spec, schema, boundary doc. No execution. The default ceiling for docs-first phases.
L1
Local replay evidence
Deterministic local execution reproduced. The ceiling for nearly all local campaigns.
L2
Reproducible benchmark artifact
Content-addressed, externally replayable artifact with provenance. Requires artifacts, never assumed.
L3
Cross-backend replay
Same result reproduced across independent backends / substrates.
L4
Formal property statement
A precise, machine-checkable property is stated (not yet proven).
L5
Machine-checked scoped proof
A scoped proof checked by a kernel/solver. A proof about one layer ≠ proof of the whole.
L6
Independently reproduced evidence
Reproduced by an independent party / implementation. The strongest claim in the system.
Hard boundaries that are never relaxed: a benchmark pass is not a proof · a local replay is not official benchmark evidence · a formal proof about one layer is not a proof of the full system · a single aggregate score must never hide weak soundness evidence · evidence ledgers are local integrity records, not tamper-proof proof systems.

✎Surface DSL → Semantic IR

Declarative benchmark families (loops, guards, state machines) lowered to an IR the local oracle can execute deterministically. Protected terminology: Surface DSL, Parsed AST, Semantic IR, Benchmark Family/Instance.

✓Oracle & Expected Verdict

A local ground-truth evaluator decides accepted/rejected traces; the Expected Verdict is preregistered so a backend can't quietly disagree with the spec.

⚠Mutation Engine

14+ adversarial mutation classes — invariant weakening/strengthening, stale-state reads, invalid unroll bounds, observation omission, witness aliasing, recursion-envelope mismatch, public/private boundary mismatch, semantic no-op drift, trace-ordering corruption — each with a declared safety class & expected verdict.

⛓Evidence Ledger

Append-only, digest-chained evidence records. Candidates → append-previews → reviewed proposals → accepted appends → materialization. Nothing becomes "accepted" without review + digest binding.

◎Distinguishability Scoring

Classifies each mutation × backend-outcome cell (true-positive, detected-rejection, unsound-acceptance, false-rejection, inconclusive) so a single aggregate score can't hide weak soundness.

▤Packs · Bundles · Audit Index

Benchmark Packs, Pack-Readiness Reports, Report Bundles, Local Audit Index, Local Benchmark Artifacts & Campaigns — content-addressed, digest-sidecared integrity bundles.

⇉Soak Runner

Deterministic shard-planned campaigns with resumable checkpoints, telemetry, health reports, and failure-corpus extraction. Internal timing telemetry is never treated as prover/verifier time.

⇪Promotion Pipeline

Candidate → review → append → materialize → official-submission package, with external-replay preflight. Every hop is fail-closed and digest-bound; promotion text is rejected by validator.

∴Formal Lane (inert seam)

FormalPropertyAssertion, FormalLaneProof, NoopFormalVerifier — a declared attach-point for real provers (SMT/Lean/COBALT), capped at L0 until a machine-checked proof exists.

SELF-MODELING RESEARCH · V17 → V41+

Astral — does the system model & improve itself?

A long-running, preregistered research program probing whether a model/agent represents and can improve its own behavior — escalated carefully from effect-explainers to perturbation-discrimination to genuine continual learning (acquire the new without forgetting the old). Every round is a named campaign with a claim ceiling.

V17–21LM effect explainers

Input-ablation & effect explainers over cached small models; establishing that local effects can be measured reproducibly before any self-model claim.

V22–23Perturbation discrimination

Activation-vs-input perturbation discrimination and higher-capability replication — can the system tell its own representations apart under controlled perturbation?

V28+Acquisition novelty

Controlled acquisition-novelty gates and PCSM-governed autoresearch provider lanes; seeds/instruments freshly generated per campaign (no reuse).

V41AGEM — continual learning

Acquisition + retention via averaged-gradient episodic-memory projection. The flagship: acquire new cases while provably retaining protected knowledge.

R27H100 campaign

Remote 20B/MXFP4/H100 48-worker qualification (16 panels × 3 seeds), sentinel-gated. Reached 30/48 before one substrate-specific acquisition failure + infra interrupt.

R28/29Local surrogate & boundary

Local MLX surrogate reproduced the mechanism per-cell (incl. the H100-failing cell), then precisely measured the substrate-capability boundary that gates full local qualification.

The AGEM result so far

Protected retention is solved by the projection (accuracy 1.0 wherever the substrate can do the protected arithmetic). Acquisition works across most cells. The one H100 failure was substrate-specific. V41R29 established that full local qualification is gated by the substrate's baseline arithmetic — a precise, measured capability boundary, not a hand-wave. This is continual learning being measured, not asserted.

⊙Sole mutation authority

pcsm_sole_state_mutation_authority is asserted in every artifact. Provider output that bypasses the journal is quarantined, not trusted.

⌗Bounded proof handoff

Cross-repo handoffs carry a digest-bound source identity, verifier status, and an explicit threshold_admitted=false until proven otherwise.

↻No-retry / no-substitution

Consumed identities are consumed. A failed cell is a retained negative, never quietly rerun or swapped — which is what makes the positives meaningful.

ECONOMIC SETTLEMENT · crates: statebook-core · statebook-settlement

Statebook — settling verified work

The closing loop: once work is evidence-bounded, it can be settled. Statebook defines terminal cash-settled contracts whose payoff is computed from evidence-bounded completeness — semantic, payoff, execution, capital, settlement, assurance, and recovery verdicts kept strictly separate, so no scalar trust score can paper over a failed critical lane.

ΣTerminal Contract IR

A scalar cash-settled terminal contract with a payoff/residual algebra. Settlement completeness is computed, not assumed.

⊟Seven completeness verdicts

Semantic · payoff · execution · capital · settlement · assurance · recovery — each Complete/Incomplete/Unknown independently. No aggregation hides a failure.

⚖Assurance-adjusted settlement

Payoff is adjusted by assurance lanes and bounded challenge/circuit-breaker contracts; independent common-numeraire valuation; atomic aggregate loss-budget reservations.

The breakthrough this builds: an economy that pays autonomous agents only for verified, evidence-bounded work — closing the loop from benchmark → identity → attestation → admission → settlement.
FORMAL LANE · Lean · SMT · COBALT

The formal-verification lane

Where claims graduate from "measured" to "machine-checked." Kept rigorously scoped: a proof about one property is never inflated to system-wide soundness. Progress so far includes an inert formal seam in zkbench-core, a Lean project (formal/hsai-gateway-digest) with a kernel-checked set-permutation-invariant theorem, and a TinyZ3/SMT execution lane for bounded arithmetic obligations.

∴Inert seam

FormalVerifier trait + NoopFormalVerifier; every proof starts DeclaredOnly, L0.

λLean kernel check

Handwritten checker-model theorem kernel-checked on a pinned Lean toolchain; shared-fixture witnesses.

⊨TinyZ3 / SMT lane

Bounded QF arithmetic obligations discharged by a local solver, quarantined & digest-bound.

▣COBALT-style containment

Future containment lane for boolean claim-boundary & small arithmetic-gate invariants.

02 / WHAT IT ALL BUILDS TOWARD

The breakthroughs

Five convergent capabilities. Each is the composition of the primitives above; none is claimed beyond its current evidence. Together they describe the end-state: autonomous systems that are safe to improve themselves because every gain is attested, journaled, bounded, and settleable.

BREAKTHROUGH 01

Evidence-bounded autonomy

An agent can only claim what it can produce evidence for, and every claim carries an enforced ceiling. This is the meta-breakthrough the entire repo demonstrates is engineerable.

via claim ladder · claim envelopes · fail-closed validators
BREAKTHROUGH 02

Proof of Agent

Autonomous workers that are attested, distinct, and economically accountable — existence you can verify, not take on faith. Attested (TEE), anchored, journaled.

via HSAI stack · Phala/dstack attestation · anchor registry
BREAKTHROUGH 03

Governed self-improvement

A research loop that improves itself while every step is journaled, evidence-chained, and ceiling-capped — the mechanism, not the model, owns state.

via PCSM autoresearch · sole-mutation-authority journals
BREAKTHROUGH 04

Measured continual learning

Acquire the new without forgetting the old, and measure it — acquisition with provable retention, on real substrates, with retained negatives.

via Astral AGEM · V41 campaigns · substrate-capability boundary
BREAKTHROUGH 05

Verifiable settlement

Pay autonomous agents only for verified work — terminal contracts whose payoff is computed from evidence-bounded completeness across seven independent lanes.

via Statebook · assurance-adjusted settlement
CAPABILITY 06

Machine-checked graduation

The path by which measured results graduate to scoped, machine-checked proofs — without ever inflating a partial proof into a system-wide soundness claim.

via formal lane · Lean kernel · TinyZ3 · COBALT containment
