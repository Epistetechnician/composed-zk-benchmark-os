The question is narrower than the name.

Astral is not a search for a single hidden feeling inside a model. It is a controlled attempt to separate three things that are easy to conflate:

what the model says,
what its state contains,
and what its state can cause.

The actor runs a task. The observer receives a defined channel. The tracer records approximate internal measurements. Then an intervention changes one part of the actor and we ask whether the observer predicted the consequence before seeing it.

That sequence gives the project a falsifiable center. Better prose is not better evidence. A signal that is easy to decode is not automatically a signal that is causally useful.

Keep the actor, observer, and effect in different rooms.

The site makes the separation visible because the research depends on it. A report can be useful without being faithful. A telemetry stream can be privileged without being causal. An intervention effect is measured outside both.
